Chicago / a personal project

My Chicago Itinerary

I like staying a little longer in the cities I visit for conferences. Chicago has always been a dream of mine, so I built myself an itinerary to make sure I could fit it all into three days.

Why I chose these colours:

  • Blue; inspired by Virgil Abloh’s Figures of Speech book.
  • Yellow; inspired by Frank Lloyd Wright’s stained glass.
  • Cherry; my take on Mies’s steel and OMA’s McCormick Tribune Campus Center at IIT.

What it does

  • Daily plans connected to Google Maps.
  • Nearby food choices and a searchable place library if I want to go off track.
  • All bookings together with my itinerary, with originals in my iCloud Drive folder.
  • A personal journal, with light and dark modes throughout.
  • An easy “open my iPhone and go” interface, just for how I like to use it :)))
