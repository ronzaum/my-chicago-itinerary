Visual provenance and recovery limits

All v2 app illustrations are fresh local renders of retained HTML or maintained source. No blurry archival screenshot is included. Sensitive travel text is removed or replaced before export. The frozen galleries contain no executable app code, document links, service credentials, journal entries or external images.

How to inspect

The article shows selected comparisons. The complete atlas links to full-size frozen HTML states and readable individual panels. Wide historical canvases scroll horizontally on a narrow display. Article screen tours are cropped to a useful opening view; their complete renders are linked in the galleries folder.

Historical colours are retained, including earlier blends and treatments that preceded the later palette restriction. This is preservation of the source design, not permission to add those colours to the current app. Article chrome is white, black and the existing neutral #e8e8e8. No repository-wide legacy palette audit is claimed.

Fonts and images

Trip Puff Open and Trip Puff Quiet are renamed local derivatives of DynaPuff. DynaPuff and its derivative font files are accompanied by fonts/dynapuff-OFL.txt. The original counter modification is documented in the project; the article does not claim authorship of the original font.

The interface reconstruction font is included with its GUST licence, manifest and derivative notices. The article itself uses system Arial. No proprietary Arial/Avenir font binaries, private photographs, map-tile screenshots, original tickets or third-party article images are bundled.
